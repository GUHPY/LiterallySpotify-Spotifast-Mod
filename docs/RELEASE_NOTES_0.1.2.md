# LiterallySpotify 0.1.2

This update brings two engine fixes from Spotifast to LiterallySpotify on Windows. The interface, animations, themes and saved preferences stay the same.

## Fixed

- **Shuffle Play on another device.** Playback starts if the active context rejects shuffle, then the app tries shuffle again on the new context. [Spotifast #1348](https://github.com/crmne/spotifast/pull/1348)
- **Names of local tracks in playlists.** Track, artist and album labels now show spaces and accents correctly, including in existing playlist caches. Playback URIs and custom names stay the same. [Spotifast #1091](https://github.com/crmne/spotifast/pull/1091)

Thanks to v14, Nikan and the Spotifast contributors.
